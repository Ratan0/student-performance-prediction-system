Student Performance Prediction System
This is a real-world Data Science + Machine Learning project where the goal is to predict which students are likely to perform poorly or fail before the final examination, so teachers can provide timely support.
1. The Problem
Schools usually discover that a student is struggling after the final exam.

For example:
A student has low attendance, performs poorly in weekly tests, submits assignments late, and has declining marks.

Instead of waiting for the final result, we want to build a system that analyzes these patterns and predicts:

“Is this student at risk of failing?”

The school can then intervene early.

9. Why Recall Is Important
This is an important real-world ML reasoning point.
Suppose there are 100 students.
There are actually 20 students who are going to fail.
Your model identifies only 10 of them.
Even if your accuracy is high, you have missed 10 students who needed help.
Therefore, for an early-warning system, Recall for the at-risk/failing class is particularly important.
You would rather have:
High Recall

than blindly optimize:
High Accuracy

because the cost of missing an at-risk student can be significant.

13. Important Real-World Issue
Be careful with data leakage.

For example, if you're trying to predict whether a student will fail before the final exam, you cannot use:
Final Exam Score

as an input feature.

Otherwise the model is essentially being given the answer.

Similarly, don't use information that would only become available after the prediction point.

A good project explicitly defines:
Prediction time = after midterm, before final examination.

Then only information available at that time should be used.
